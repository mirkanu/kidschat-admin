<objective>
Overhaul the daily-summary email so each per-child card becomes actionable rather than noisy: drop imageRequests and Presets Used, add an Alerts section (count + AI paraphrase when > 0), and add a per-kid AI chat summary (1 sentence + Concerns line). All AI calls go through `@anthropic-ai/sdk` on Haiku 4.5, billed to the kidschat-admin service's ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (keeps the kid-facing budget clean).

Purpose: The current email tells the parent a kid sent N messages and used certain presets — it doesn't tell them what the kid actually talked about or whether anything concerning came up. After this change, the email becomes a genuine daily awareness tool: one glance tells the parent their kid's topics, alert count, and whether to check in.

Output: A deployed, smoke-tested daily-summary email matching the CONTEXT.md spec, with graceful degradation if Anthropic is down.
