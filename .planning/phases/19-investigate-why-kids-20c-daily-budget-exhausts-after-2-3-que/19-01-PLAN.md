<objective>
Apply the quick-win librechat.yaml changes that eliminate the largest per-turn overheads identified in Area 3 and Area 2 of the research, and close the new-account startBalance loophole identified in Open Question #4.

Purpose: The research confirms ~2,580 tokens of DALL-E tool schema are attached to EVERY request (including pure text) because all 4 presets enable the DALL-E tool. Removing the tool from text presets cuts every text turn's prompt cost by ~2,580 credits. Adding a maxContextTokens cap stops history compounding. Fixing startBalance stops new accounts from bypassing the daily cap.

Output: A new Gist revision of librechat.yaml plus a redeployed LibreChat service pinned to that revision. No application code changes in this plan.
</objective>
