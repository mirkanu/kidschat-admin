### Summary

Phase 15.4 fully achieves its goal. All four v2.4 audit gaps are closed:

1. **IMG-LIMITS-03** — `monthlySpendEur` is now a live field. `accumulateYesterdaySpend` increments it atomically via `$inc` at every midnight UTC cron fire. `displayedMonthlySpendEur` adds today's partial spend for real-time display in `UsageBars`.

2. **IMG-ENFORCE-02** — Monthly cap enforcement is real. `topUpDailyBudget` checks `displayedMonthlySpendEur >= monthlyCostCapEur` and skips the token refill entirely when exhausted, while still advancing `lastDailyReset` to prevent re-evaluation loops.

4. **IMG-SAFETY-04** — `/api/notify/safety-alert` now accepts `alertType=image_prompt`. The `VALID_ALERT_TYPES` const array is the single source of truth for the whitelist.
