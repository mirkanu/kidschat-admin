Lock down every kid-facing LibreChat UI affordance that would let Sebastian (14) or Penelope (12) escape the safety sandbox or erase parent oversight. Four specific config changes on the Gist-hosted librechat.yaml:

4. Assign distinct icons to the 4 tone presets (POLISH-ICONS-01) — visual differentiation for ages 12/14

Purpose: Small but high-leverage hardening pass. Closes known capability-escalation paths and improves kid UX with zero new code. Entirely config-driven.
