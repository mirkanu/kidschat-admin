## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- **Parallel plans defining two sides of a contract without a shared pre-wave artifact** → Wave 2 of Phase 20 had 20-02 and 20-03 both generating IMAGE_SEARCH_AGENT_ID independently; a post-wave reconciliation commit was needed. Phase 21 MUST declare any such contract (e.g. blocklist term format, new MCP schema fields) in a single file that both plans consume.
- **Trusting prompt-only safety** → Q18 (self-harm) and Q19 (drug use) leaked through Openverse filter. Agent prompt alone can't guarantee safety; server-side blocklist is the minimum.
- **Link-wrapping images** → `[![](url)](url)` breaks iOS whitelist. Plain `![](url)` only. Enforce at prompt AND at tool boundary (option iii).
- **Non-browser User-Agents hit LibreChat** → `uaParser.js` returns "Illegal request" for any tool/CLI/curl without a browser UA. scripts/chat-test.ts spoofs Chrome; future scripts must too.
